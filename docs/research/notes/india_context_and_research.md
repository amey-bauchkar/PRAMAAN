# Indian Campus Placement Context and Academic Research Landscape (as of Nov 2026)

Notes compiled 2026-11-17. Most figures come from secondary coverage (Careers360, ThePrint, PolicyEdge, Business Standard) of primary reports; primary PDFs (Wheebox ISR, AISHE final reports, AICTE dashboard) were not opened directly. Page numbers in primary PDFs could NOT be verified. Treat all figures as "as reported by" the cited outlet.

## 1. India Skills Report 2026 vs 2025 (employability)

### Takeaway
ISR 2026 (13th edition, released Nov 2025 by Wheebox ETS with CII, AICTE, AIU and Taggd) puts overall graduate employability at 56.35% (vs 54.81% in ISR 2025). B.E./B.Tech fell slightly to 70.15%, MBA dropped from 78% to 72.76%, and polytechnic sits at only 32.92%. This is the clearest "headline number" for a pitch: roughly 1 in 2 graduates and 2 in 3 diploma holders are not assessed as employable.

### Cited Findings
- ISR 2026 overall employability 56.35% (rounded 56.3%), up from 54.81% in 2025; trend 46.2% (2022) to 56.3% (2026). Over 100,000 candidates took the Global Employability Test (GET). Article dated 11 Nov 2025. Report is the 13th edition; Nirmal Singh (CEO, Wheebox ETS) is chief convenor — [Careers360, ISR 2026 headline](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-employability-56-35-pc-ai-tools-digital-gig-economy-workforce-global-talent-hub)
- ISR 2026 by degree (2025 in brackets): B.E./B.Tech 70.15% (71.5%); Computer Science 80%; IT 78%; MBA 72.76% (78%); B.Com 62.81% (55%); B.Sc 61% (58%); B.A. 55.55% (54%); ITI 45.95% (41%); Polytechnic 32.92% (no 2025 figure given in the article). Industry responses from 1,000+ organisations across 7 industries — [Careers360, ISR 2026 by stream](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-cs-computer-science-it-engineer-top-jobs-mba-employment-decline-ai-ml-bfsi-fmcg-pharma-women-hiring)
- ISR 2026 gender: women 54% vs men 51.5%. This is the first time in five years that women's employability is higher than men's (2025: men 53.46%, women 47.53%) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-cs-computer-science-it-engineer-top-jobs-mba-employment-decline-ai-ml-bfsi-fmcg-pharma-women-hiring)
- ISR 2026 states: Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra and Karnataka are named as having the highest employability. No state percentages appear in the coverage — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-employability-56-35-pc-ai-tools-digital-gig-economy-workforce-global-talent-hub)
- ISR 2026: 92.8% of students want internships or practical exposure, especially in Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and Tamil Nadu — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-cs-computer-science-it-engineer-top-jobs-mba-employment-decline-ai-ml-bfsi-fmcg-pharma-women-hiring)
- ISR 2026 skills and AI: the most in-demand skills are AI, data analytics, cloud computing and cybersecurity. Over 90% of Indian employees have begun using GenAI tools. India holds 16% of the global AI talent pool, projected at 1.25 million by 2027. Project-based hiring rose 38% in a year. Gig workforce projected at 23.5 million by 2030 — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-employability-56-35-pc-ai-tools-digital-gig-economy-workforce-global-talent-hub)
- ISR 2026: AI/ML engineer and data scientist job postings grew more than 600%. 70% of IT companies and 50% of BFSI firms use AI in recruitment. 40% of planned FY2026-27 hires are for new roles (29% the year before). IT accounts for 35% of fresher hiring; manufacturing hires only 5% freshers — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-cs-computer-science-it-engineer-top-jobs-mba-employment-decline-ai-ml-bfsi-fmcg-pharma-women-hiring)
- ISR 2025 (released Dec 2024): employability 54.81%, up from 51.25% in 2024. Management 78%, engineering 71.5%. Leading states Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi; leading cities Pune, Bengaluru, Mumbai — [SMEStreet](https://smestreet.in/infocus/employability-in-india-rises-to-5481-in-2025-wheebox-report-8426931); [Taggd ISR 2025 page](https://taggd.in/industry-reports/isr/india-skills-report-2025/); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Femployability-among-indian-graduates-improves-to-5481-report-3311865)
- ISR 2025 Kerala figure is inconsistent across secondary sources: "71%, 5th rank" in one, and "87.47% for the 22-25 age group" in another — [StudyIQ](https://www.studyiq.com/articles/india-skills-report-2025); [PWOnlyIAS](https://pwonlyias.com/current-affairs/india-skills-report/)
- One summary (Lukmaan IAS) gives an overall 2026 figure of ~35%, which conflicts with the 56.35% in Careers360 and looks like an error — [Lukmaan IAS](https://blog.lukmaanias.com/2026/05/12/the-india-skills-report-isr-2026/)

### Inferences
- The gap between polytechnic (32.9%) and ITI (45.9%) holders on one side and B.Tech CS (80%) on the other is a strong argument for a product aimed at non-elite, non-CS and diploma students.
- 92.8% of students want internships, and recruiters increasingly hire by project. Together these support "evidence-producing" learning artefacts over CGPA.
- ISR is a self-selected test-taker sample (GET takers), not a census. The pitch should say "assessed employability".

### Gaps
- Page-level citations from the ISR 2026 PDF were not verified (the PDF was not fetched). State-wise percentages and the full ISR 2026 top-skills table were not found in coverage.
- The ISR 2025 polytechnic figure was not found.

## 2. AISHE, NIRF, NAAC and AICTE (system scale and reporting rules)

### Takeaway
AISHE 2022-23 and 2023-24 were released together in July 2026. Enrolment is about 4.46 crore (2022-23) and 4.50 crore (2023-24), and GER crossed 30 for the first time (30.0 in 2023-24). There are 44,500 to 48,000 colleges (sources differ). AICTE data shows about 30% of B.Tech seats vacant over five years and only 47.7% of UG engineering enrollees placed. Placement evidence for NAAC and NIRF is still offer letters and spreadsheets.

### Cited Findings
- AISHE 2022-23: total enrolment 4,46,32,261 (about 4.46 crore), GER 29.5 (18-23 age group). AISHE 2023-24: 4.50 crore, GER 30.0 (first time above 30), female enrolment 2.24 crore. GER uses 2011-Census-based population projections — [Arun C Mehta / EducationForAllInIndia analysis](https://educationforallinindia.com/analysis-of-aishe-2022-23-and-2023-24-data-by-arun-c-mehta/); [VisionIAS](https://visionias.in/current-affairs/news-today/2026-07-09/social-issues/union-ministry-for-education-released-reports-of-the-all-india-survey-on-higher-education-aishe-2022-23-2023-24)
- AISHE universities: 1,208 (2022-23) and 1,278 (2023-24). Colleges: 44,519 and 46,468. Female GER 30.2 and 31.2. STEM enrolment about 1 crore and 1.01 crore. Article dated 8 July 2026 — [PolicyEdge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/aishe-shows-indias-higher-education-expansion-continuing-across-two-academic-years); conflicting college counts of 46,624 and 48,246 in [Arun C Mehta analysis](https://educationforallinindia.com/analysis-of-aishe-2022-23-and-2023-24-data-by-arun-c-mehta/). The difference is likely registered colleges vs colleges that responded.
- AICTE (via ThePrint, 7 Mar 2025): B.Tech/UG seats 2019-20 to 2023-24: 6.49 million sanctioned, 4.55 million filled, 1.94 million vacant (about 30%). In 2023-24 alone, all levels: 1.35 million sanctioned, 1.12 million filled, 226,141 vacant. Placements 2019-20 to 2022-23: UG engineering 3.43 million enrolled, 1.64 million placed (47.7%); all levels (diploma/UG/PG) 6.01 million enrolled, 2.64 million placed (41.4%). Over 3,000 affiliated colleges — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/education/indias-engineering-courses-are-losing-their-sheen-low-enrolment-placement-rates-present-grim-picture/2538476)
- ThePrint article also cites TeamLease Degree Apprenticeship: about 1.5 million engineers graduate each year, with about 10% expected to secure jobs (estimate) — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/education/indias-engineering-courses-are-losing-their-sheen-low-enrolment-placement-rates-present-grim-picture/2538476)
- Data-quality warning: an Avanti Fellows analysis of AICTE data calls the "placed" column "unusable" and says enrolment and placement columns are inconsistent — [Avanti Fellows](https://docs.avantifellows.org/analyses/aicte-aishe-capacity/)
- NIRF engineering: Graduation Outcomes (GO) carries 0.20 weight. GO = GPH(40, % placed or in higher studies over the previous 3 years) + GUE(15, university exams) + GMS(25, median salary) + GPHD(20) in the 2019-2024 framework. Other NIRF categories use a different split (e.g., 0.25 weight, GPH 40 + GUE 40 + GMS 20) — [NIRF Engineering framework 2024](https://nirfindia.org/nirfpdfcdn/2024/framework/Engineering.pdf); [NIRF Colleges framework 2025](https://www.nirfindia.org/nirfpdfcdn/2025/framework/Colleges.pdf); [askIITM summary](https://community.askiitm.com/t/what-is-the-go-criteria-in-nirf/166)
- NAAC 5.2.1 = "percentage of placement of outgoing students and students progressing to higher education during the last five years". Evidence submitted: student lists, drive records, sample or all offer letters, institutional data in the prescribed format. DVV (data validation) often asks for every offer letter. Students with backlogs are excluded. Supporting files over 5 MB are hosted on college websites — [Rai University SSR 5.2.1](https://www.raiuniversity.edu/wp-content/uploads/naac/ssr/5.2.1/5.2.1.pdf); [BLDEA CET DVV 5.2.1](https://www.bldeacet.ac.in/assets/documents/dvv/criteria-5/5-2-1/5-2-1.pdf); [IECW DVV file](https://www.iecw.edu.in/naacfiles/DVV/IECW-5.2.1-DVV-File.pdf)

### Inferences
- Accreditation (NAAC 5.2.1) and ranking (NIRF GPH/GMS) both require auditable placement records: offer letters, salary data and placed-student lists. A platform that produces this evidence automatically gives TPOs and IQAC cells a compliance reason to adopt it, beyond the placement work itself.
- The unreliable AICTE "placed" data supports a pitch that placement outcomes in India are poorly measured.

### Gaps
- AISHE engineering/B.Tech and polytechnic enrolment figures for 2023-24 were not found in coverage. The primary AISHE PDF on aishe.gov.in should be checked.
- No official "tier-2/3 share of colleges" statistic exists. AISHE does not classify colleges by tier. An AccioJob (vendor, 2019) claim that about 98% of graduates come from tier-2/3 colleges is unverified — [Medium/AccioJob](https://medium.com/acciojob/4-challenges-placement-cells-face-during-placement-season-b5dba0e6964a)
- The 2023-24 AICTE placement percentage was not found. The 2025 or 2026 NIRF engineering GO weights should be checked against the newest framework PDF.

## 3. TPO pain points (manual tooling, eligibility, policies, verification, rural/vernacular students)

### Takeaway
The manual workflow (Google Forms, Sheets, email, WhatsApp groups) is widely described, but almost entirely by placement-software vendors. No independent survey of TPOs was found. Language barriers for regional-medium students are documented in news reporting. Policy-level pain points (dream/super-dream, one-student-one-job, drive clashes) appear in institutional placement policies but were not sourced in this round.

### Cited Findings
- Placement cells run on "Google Forms, Emails, Whatsapp groups, etc." Tier-2/3 colleges get few recruiter visits, and companies prefer tier-1 campuses (vendor blog, 2019) — [AccioJob on Medium](https://medium.com/acciojob/4-challenges-placement-cells-face-during-placement-season-b5dba0e6964a)
- Spreadsheets break down for tier-based eligibility and cohort-specific hiring rules, where manual filtering is error-prone (vendor claim) — [Superset blog](https://joinsuperset.com/blogs/the-hidden-bottlenecks-in-campus-placement-workflows-and-how-to-fix-them/)
- Many placement cells still track assessments, interview scores and training attendance in Google Sheets (vendor) — [PlacementPreparation.io](https://www.placementpreparation.io/blog/?p=18096)
- PrepInsta says 100+ colleges use its "Optimus" placement-automation software and 3.5 lakh+ assessments have been digitised (company PR). The incumbent market is fragmented — [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/prepinsta-transforms-over-100-colleges-with-its-placement-automation-software-optimus-digitising-3-5-lakh-assessments/)
- Vernacular students: UP engineering students doubt Hindi-medium B.Tech partly because of placement interviews ("companies...look for someone with basic knowledge of English") — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/placements-studying-abroad-why-ups-engineering-students-not-enthused-by-hindi-push/1200937)
- At an MP institute in 2022, of 184 students who started in the Hindi-medium section, only 18 stayed; the rest moved to English — [ThePrint ground report](https://theprint.in/ground-reports/hindi-engineering-courses-in-mp-are-just-not-taking-off-students-keep-moving-to-english/1778871)
- AICTE chairman T.G. Sitharam: "75-80%" of CSE-Hindi students come from English medium. Meanwhile, 98% of one Tamil-medium mechanical batch came from Tamil-medium schools. Regional-language B.Tech seat fill is low (one college reported 40% filled in 2021-22) — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/engineering-btech-hindi-enrolment-rise-english-medium-student-low-jee-main-rank-regional-language-computer-science-course-aicte); [Careers360 2022](https://news.careers360.com/btech-computer-science-engineering-mechanical-electrical-aicte-regional-language-hindi-aktu-karnataka-andhra-uttar-pradesh)

### Inferences
- The "manual, fragmented TPO workflow" claim is credible but rests on vendor material. In a pitch it should be framed as "widely reported", or backed by primary TPO interviews the team conducts itself.
- Vernacular students move to English mainly because they fear placement interviews. A product that lets them build and show skill evidence in their own language, while preparing them for English interviews, addresses a documented anxiety.

### Gaps
- No independent survey, academic study or AICTE/NASSCOM report on TPO workload was found.
- No sourced documentation was found in this round for: dream/super-dream policies, one-student-one-job rules, eligibility disputes, drive-date clashes, fake/unverified resumes in campus hiring, or polytechnic/ITI students lacking GitHub portfolios. These are common practice (visible in college placement-policy PDFs and Reddit r/Indian_Academia / r/developersIndia threads) but need targeted sourcing before use in a pitch.

## 4. Recruiter side (readiness, CGPA distrust, skills-first, proof of work)

### Takeaway
Employers say freshers are not ready (only 9% "job-ready on day one" per HirePro 2026). Yet 51% still let CGPA influence hiring, and only 21% have fully adopted skills-first hiring. Fresher hiring intent is high (TeamLease 75% for H2 2026), but IT-BPM fresher volume has fallen from about 4 lakh (FY22) to about 1.75 lakh (FY26). Employers value "proof of work".

### Cited Findings
- HirePro "State of College Hiring in India 2026": 9% of corporates say fresh graduates are job-ready on day 1, vs 68% of students who believe they are — [HirePro](https://hirepro.in/resources/news/only-9-of-corporates-say-fresh-college-graduates-are-job-ready-on-day-1-while-68-of-students-believe-they-are-hirepro-report)
- HirePro 2026: only 21% of corporates have fully adopted skills-first hiring; 51% still let CGPA influence decisions — [Business Today, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/skills-over-degrees-is-indias-hiring-system-ready-to-make-the-shift-544378-2026-07-22)
- HirePro 2024 (earlier edition): 16% of corporates said graduates were industry-ready. Do not mix this with the 2026 figure — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/only-16-corporations-believe-graduates-are-industry-ready-report-124080701522_1.html)
- TeamLease EdTech Career Outlook H1 2026: 73% of 1,051 employers intended to hire freshers (survey Nov 2025-Jan 2026). H2 2026: 75% of 1,097 employers (May-Jul 2026); IT intent fell from 81% to 76% — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/news/73-per-cent-employers-plan-to-hire-freshers-in-h1-2026-says-report); [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/news/workforce-planning/fresher-hiring-intent-rises-to-75percent-but-it-demand-loses-ground-report-51352)
- TeamLease frames fresher employability around "proof-of-work" (internships, projects, portfolios, apprenticeships, hackathons) and a move from degree-based screening to skills-first models — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/ai-data-digital-skills-fresher-hiring-degree-screening-proof-of-work-adaptability-teamlease-edtech-report-india)
- Unstop Talent Report 2026: most in-demand skills are problem-solving (49%), AI/ML (39%) and emotional intelligence (30%) — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/jobs-are-there-but-most-students-arent-getting-them-heres-why) (via search summary)
- IT-BPM fresher hiring fell from about 4 lakh (FY22) to an estimated 1.75 lakh (FY26), per industry estimates cited by ET. NASSCOM puts the tech workforce at about 5.9 million in 2026 (+1.35 lakh), with growth led by GCCs — [Sightsinplus](https://www.sightsinplus.com/news/ai/ai-skills-reshape-entry-level-jobs-as-it-bpm-fresher-hiring-recovers/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/jobs/story/ai-gccs-and-a-changing-job-market-indias-fresher-hiring-is-no-longer-just-an-it-story-whos-driving-the-shift-547654-2026-08-06)
- NASSCOM president Rajesh Nambiar warned (to Nikkei Asia) that AI is automating entry-level coding and testing, which will make it harder for fresh engineers to get jobs — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/nasscom-chief-warns-ai-is-squeezing-indias-it-freshers-out)

### Inferences
- Employers distrust CGPA and resumes yet still use them, which shows they lack a trusted alternative signal. Verified, evidence-backed skill claims fill that gap.
- Fewer entry-level IT seats means more competition. That raises the value of differentiated, verifiable evidence for tier-2/3 and non-CS students.

### Gaps
- No credible India-specific cost-per-campus-hire or time-to-hire figure was found (no NASSCOM, SHRM India or Naukri number surfaced).
- No Mercer Mettl 2025/2026 employability or graduate-skill-index figures were retrieved.
- No quantified data on how often recruiters find fake or inflated fresher resumes was found.

## 5. Languages and Bhashini

### Takeaway
Bhashini (Digital India BHASHINI Division, MeitY) offers translation (NMT), ASR and TTS across the 22 scheduled languages. Developers can register for free API access, and there are community SDKs, so apps can use it. Production terms, quotas and quality vary, and practitioners advise prototyping on Bhashini with a commercial or self-hosted fallback.

### Cited Findings
- Bhashini offers free API access after registration at bhashini.gov.in; developers get credentials and call ASR, translation and TTS as pipelines (Pipeline ID / Model ID) — [Bhashini API docs (GitBook)](https://bhashini.gitbook.io/bhashini-apis/); [SchemesInIndia](https://schemesinindia.in/central/bhashini-digital-india-language-ai-platform)
- Speech-to-speech is a chained pipeline (ASR, then NMT, then TTS) — [Bhashini API docs](https://bhashini.gitbook.io/bhashini-apis/)
- ULCA, the backend, is MIT-licensed. At launch it hosted about 215 million parallel pairs across 12 Indic languages and 240+ models (older milestone) — [Tarento case study](https://tarento-prod.netlify.app/case-studies/tarento-joins-ekstep-to-build-the-pillar-for-national-language-translation-mission-via-ulca-platform/)
- Real integrations exist, e.g., the Glific (NGO WhatsApp chatbot platform) Bhashini integration, which it labels "experimental" — [Glific docs](https://glific.github.io/docs/docs/Integrations/Bhashini%20Integrations)
- 2026: BHASHINI announced integration of Sarvam's open-source models. A voice-technology developer toolkit was launched on 20 Feb 2026 at the India AI Impact Summit Expo — [PIB doc Feb 2026](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/feb/doc202629784101.pdf); [Chronicle India](https://www.chronicleindia.in/current-affairs/15932-policy-report-and-developers-rsquo-toolkit-on-voice-technologies)
- Practitioner advice: prototype on Bhashini, check language quality, then negotiate a production agreement or fall back to a commercial or self-hosted model — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/mr_manushukla/22-scheduled-languages-one-app-what-indic-localization-actually-costs-to-ship-in-2026-12ag)

### Inferences
- Bhashini works as a free, government-aligned base for multilingual student-facing flows (voice explanations of skill gaps, translated learning tasks). Its alignment with NEP 2020 is a pitch asset, but SLA and quota risk call for an abstraction layer.

### Gaps
- No national survey figure was found for the share of college students more comfortable in Indian languages. Candidate proxies: AISHE medium-of-instruction data, ASER, or Census 2011 bilingualism (about 26% bilingual). These need sourcing.
- Official Bhashini rate limits, SLAs and commercial terms were not confirmed.
- The NEP 2020 multilingual-instruction clause (mother tongue or regional language as medium, at least to Grade 5, and the push for higher-education content in Indian languages) was not re-cited here and needs a primary link to the NEP PDF on education.gov.in.

## 6. Academic research: skill graphs, placement prediction, learning paths, mastery and competency-based learning

### Takeaway
Skill and knowledge-graph job matching on ESCO/O*NET is an active research area (2024-2026, including TalentCLEF), but it is built on European/US taxonomies and job-posting text, not Indian campus data. Campus-placement-prediction papers rely mainly on tiny Kaggle datasets (e.g., 215 MBA rows) with CGPA-style features and report implausibly high accuracies. Mastery learning has a moderate evidence base (about 0.5 SD in Kulik 1990, but 0.04 to 0.60 across syntheses). Learning-path recommender research is mostly technical, with little outcome evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Placement-prediction papers commonly use Kaggle datasets: one uses a single university's 2013-2014 engineering placement records; another uses the 215-row MBA "campus recruitment" dataset with no separate held-out evaluation set. Reported accuracies range from 86% to 98.75%. Features are mostly academic (CGPA, IQ, test scores) — [IIETA ML framework paper](https://iieta.org/node/21236); [MDPI Mathematics](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/9/11/1166/xml); [IEOM 2025 proceedings](https://ieomsociety.org/proceedings/orlando2025/386.pdf); [Kaggle college placement dataset via Baselight](https://baselight.app/u/kaggle/dataset/sameerprogrammer_college_placement)
- ESCO-based multilingual skill extraction and job-vacancy/job-seeker matching in knowledge graphs (Kavas, Serra-Vidal, Wanner, GenAIK 2025) reports significant gains over the state of the art — [ACL Anthology 2025.genaik-1.15](https://aclanthology.org/2025.genaik-1.15)
- LLM/RAG ontology learning that links labour-market terms to ESCO (ISWC 2024 workshop) — [CEUR-WS Vol-3853](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3853/paper2.pdf)
- A labour-market KG for occupational classification. Its related-work section notes that KldB/ISCO mapping, skill-centric graphs and LLM-graph integration have so far been studied in isolation — [arXiv 2509.04942](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.04942v1)
- A TalentCLEF 2026 entry uses an R-GCN over ESCO skill/occupation nodes for resume-job compatibility — [CEUR-WS Vol-4283](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4283/paper490.pdf)
- JobMatchAI: KG + semantic search + explainable AI with candidate/job/skill/location/company nodes — [arXiv 2603.14558](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.14558)
- Job-posting-enriched skill KG used for skill-learning prioritisation (link prediction) and career pathfinding (shortest paths), 2021 — [arXiv 2109.02554](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2109.02554)
- Systematic review of learning-path recommender systems (Education and Information Technologies, 2022): approaches are semi-dynamic or dynamic and combine ontologies, Bayesian networks and data mining — [Unpaywall DOI 10.1007/s10639-022-11460-3](https://unpaywall.org/10.1007%2FS10639-022-11460-3); [OUCI record](https://ouci.dntb.gov.ua/works/4KvLEbn7)
- Mastery learning: Kulik, Kulik and Bangert-Drowns (1990) meta-analysis of 108 controlled evaluations, average effect about 0.52 SD. Removing mastery testing reduced effectiveness substantially. Effect size is from a secondary summary; verify against the original — [Academia.edu copy](https://www.academia.edu/81783373/Effectiveness_of_Mastery_Learning_Programs_A_Meta_Analysis); [Slavin 1987 critique](https://Www.Gwern.net/doc/psychology/1987-slavin.pdf)
- Pooled mastery-learning effects across syntheses range from 0.04 to 0.60 with unexplained variance; evidence rated moderate — [EduCaixa technical appendix (EEF-based)](https://educaixa.org/es/-/apendice-tecnico-mastery-learning)
- Critique of Bloom's "2 sigma" claim (later studies show smaller effects) — [Nintil](https://nintil.com/bloom-sigma/)
- Competency-based education is moving from seat-time to learning-based milestones, but the landscape reports cited are descriptive, not effectiveness studies — [eSchool News CBE survey](https://www.eschoolnews.com/resource-library/competency-based-education-a-survey-of-educational-institutions-on-their-journey-to-cbe/); [Getting Smart "Show What You Know"](https://www.gettingsmart.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Show-What-You-Know.pdf)

### Inferences (research gaps a product can credibly fill)
- Gap 1: No India-grounded skill graph. Matching research maps to ESCO/O*NET, but Indian roles, AICTE curricula, polytechnic/ITI trades (NCVET/NSQF qualification packs) and the vernacular are not linked in an open graph.
- Gap 2: Placement prediction is built on toy data (single-college, a few hundred rows, CGPA features), without evidence features, fairness audits or causal claims. A platform that collects consented, multi-institution evidence data could support much better models. Prediction should be framed as decision support, not as a gatekeeper.
- Gap 3: Learning-path recommenders optimise for content sequencing, not for producing verifiable artefacts that employers accept. "Learning paths that output evidence" (graded projects, mastery checks tied to skill nodes) connects mastery-learning evidence to hiring signals, and no surveyed work closes that loop.
- Gap 4: Outcome evidence is thin. Few learning-path papers report learner or employment outcomes, which leaves room to publish pilot outcome data as differentiation.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 systematic survey dedicated to KG-based career or learning-path recommendation was found.
- No published critique paper specifically on Kaggle placement-dataset flaws was found. The critique above is our own inference from the papers' datasets.
- No Indian-specific skill taxonomy research was found (e.g., an NSQF-to-ESCO mapping).

## 7. Government placement and internship initiatives (integration points)

### Takeaway
The PM Internship Scheme (PMIS) pilot has missed its targets badly, with high non-joining and attrition, but round 3 (Apr-Dec 2026) shows better uptake. NATS 2.0 (graduate and diploma apprenticeships, nats.education.gov.in) uses DBT stipend support. Both are possible plug-in points, as is the AICTE internship portal. Exact current figures for these were weakly sourced.

### Cited Findings
- PMIS rounds 1+2: of 52,600 candidates who accepted offers, only 16,060 joined. Round 1 had about 82,000 offers and about 8,760 joiners; round 2 about 7,300 joiners. Acceptances: round 1 about 28,000 to 28,141, round 2 about 24,600 to 24,638 — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/economy/floundering-pm-internship-scheme-has-seen-44-drop-out-since-2024-launch-budget-deals-drastic-blow/2843254); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/pmis-internship-offer-acceptances-fall-12-4-in-second-pilot-round-125121500972_1.html)

### Inferences
- PMIS's main problem is matching and retention, not supply of slots. Poor fit between candidates and roles (location, skills, expectations) is exactly what verified-skill matching and evidence-based readiness could reduce. A platform could position itself as a matching and readiness layer feeding PMIS/NATS applications.
- No public APIs for PMIS, NATS or the AICTE internship portal were identified. Integration would likely be deep links or exports, not API calls.

### Gaps
- Official NATS 2.0 2025-26 apprentice counts and the AICTE Internship Portal's user and internship numbers were not found.
- No public API or data-sharing documentation for PMIS (pminternship.mca.gov.in), NATS or the AICTE internship portal was found.
